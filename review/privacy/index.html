Axemacana Review privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026. Axe Macana is run by Zack Robertson. Contact: axemacana@gmail.com. This policy covers Axemacana Review, the tool we use to show videos from our Google Drive to the people we work with, so they can watch them and leave comments at points in the video.

What we access

Only the video files we pick one at a time in Google's file picker. The app asks for Google's drive.file permission, which covers those picked files and nothing else in the Drive. For each picked file we read its name, type, size and the video itself.

We never see or store your Google password.

How we use it

When someone with access to a review presses play, the video is streamed from Google Drive to them. The video passes through our server and is not saved there. We keep the file's Drive ID and name so the review can find the video again, and the comments people write on it.

We never sell Google user data, never use it for advertising, never share it with anyone except the people we have given access to that review, and never use it to train AI models.

Axemacana Review's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where it is kept

The Google access key, the file IDs and names, and the comments are stored on Axe Macana's own private server, not in a public cloud service. The access key is stored encrypted and is used only to fetch the picked files.

How long we keep it

Until we disconnect Drive or you ask us to delete it. When you ask, we delete the stored data within 30 days. The videos themselves stay in Google Drive.

How to disconnect

  • Remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Or email us and we will do it for you.
