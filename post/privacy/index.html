Axe Macana Publishing privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026. Axe Macana is run by Zack Robertson. Contact: axemacana@gmail.com. This policy covers the tool we use to schedule and publish content to your social accounts. Terms of service.

What we access

Only what you allow when you connect an account:

  • TikTok: your profile name, picture and public statistics, the list of your public videos, and permission to upload and publish videos to your account.
  • YouTube, Instagram and X: your profile name and picture, and permission to publish posts and videos to your account.

We never see or store your password.

How we use it

To schedule and publish the content that you or your team have approved, to the accounts you connected, and to show whether each post was published. We never sell your data, never use it for advertising, and never use it to train AI models.

Where it is kept

The access key, your profile details, and the posts and video files waiting to be published are stored on Axe Macana's own private server, not in a public cloud service. Only the people working on your account can see them. A video file is reachable by the platform through a private link only so that the platform can fetch it for publishing.

How long we keep it

Until you disconnect or ask us to delete it. When you do, we delete the access key and your stored data within 30 days. Content already published stays on your own account; you can remove it there.

How to disconnect

  • TikTok: in the TikTok app, open Settings and privacy, then Security and permissions, then Manage app permissions, and remove "Axe Macana".
  • YouTube: remove the app at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Or email us and we will do it for you.
