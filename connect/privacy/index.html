Axe Macana privacy policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026. Axe Macana is run by Zack Robertson. Contact: axemacana@gmail.com.

What we read

Only what you allow when you connect, and only to report on your own content:

  • YouTube (Google permission "View YouTube Analytics reports for your YouTube content"): analytics reports for your channel and videos, such as views, watch time, audience retention and traffic sources.
  • Instagram (Meta permissions instagram_basic and instagram_manage_insights): your profile name and follower count, your posts with their date, link, likes and comments, and post insights such as views, reach, saves and shares.

Access is read-only. We cannot post, edit, delete, send messages or change any setting. We never see or store your password.

How we use it

To produce reports for you and for the team that manages your content: which posts did well, and when. We may use AI tools to summarise these numbers for you. We never sell your data, never use it for advertising, and never use it to train AI models.

Where it is kept

The access key and the numbers are stored on Axe Macana's own private server, not in a public cloud service, and never in your Google Drive. Only the people working on your account can see them.

How long we keep it

Until you disconnect or ask us to delete it. When you do, we delete the access key and your stored numbers within 30 days.

How to disconnect

  • YouTube: remove "Axe Macana" at myaccount.google.com/connections.
  • Instagram: remove "Axe Macana" from your business's connected apps in Meta Business Suite settings.
  • Or email us and we will do it for you.

Google user data

Axe Macana's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
