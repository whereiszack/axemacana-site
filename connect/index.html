Axe Macana helps creators and brands understand how their posts perform. When you connect your YouTube channel or Instagram account, Axe Macana reads your post numbers (views, watch time, reach, likes, comments, saves and shares) so we can report back to you on what works.

Access is read-only. Axe Macana cannot post, edit, delete, message or change anything on your accounts, and we never see your password. You can remove access at any time.
